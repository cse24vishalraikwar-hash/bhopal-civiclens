About CivicLens

Bhopal CivicLens is a geospatial exploration project that helps people discover public facilities across Bhopal. It combines interactive mapping, category-based discovery and location data analysis. This prototype is a learning project; listings may be incomplete and should be independently verified before use in an emergency.
