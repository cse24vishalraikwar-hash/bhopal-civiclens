About CivicLens

Bhopal CivicLens is a geospatial exploration project that helps people discover important public facilities across Bhopal. It combines interactive mapping, category-based discovery and location data analysis.

The project aims to make public information easier to explore and provide a starting point for understanding how essential services are distributed.
